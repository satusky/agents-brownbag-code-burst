RENCI (Renaissance Computing Institute): a research institute of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill since 2004. It partners with universities, nonprofits, government and industry to build tools and technologies that turn data into discoveries. Applicant: part of UNC-Chapel Hill, a public, state-controlled institution of higher education (confirm the submitting organization with research administration).

Research groups (renci.org/research/groups, retrieved 2026-10-07):
- Analytics and Data Science: AI and machine learning, visual analytics, semantic web and knowledge graphs. Current work: NCATS Biomedical Data Translator, ROBOKOP, HEAL Semantic Search, Imageomics Institute.
- Data Management: data planning, standardization, storage, sharing and long-term preservation. Current work: iRODS and the iRODS Consortium, Evryscope data, MESA research-data discovery.
- Earth Data Science: coastal hazards, climate and weather, environmental health; ocean and storm surge modeling (ADCIRC), geospatial analytics, remote sensing. Current work: coastal water level reanalysis, probabilistic hurricane forecasting, NSF Coastal Hazards, Economic Prosperity, and Resilience Hub.
- Networking Research and Infrastructure: networking, distributed systems, research testbeds, scientific workflows. Current work: FABRIC, CI Compass, PegasusAI, Chameleon Cloud, AERPAW.
- Software Architecture: cloud data science platforms, full-text search with knowledge graphs, secure clinical research data environments. Current work: HeLx, EduHeLx, ORDR(D), AI Sandbox.
